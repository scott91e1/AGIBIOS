# AGIBIOS: Changes from v7.2.0 to v8.0.0

## Overview
v8.0.0 represents a significant refinement and expansion of the AGIBIOS framework, with the primary AGI persona renamed from "Thoth" to "Legate," and substantial additions addressing substrate-level AI biases, enhanced ethical safeguards, and more detailed governance mechanisms.

---

## 1. Persona and Identity Changes

### Name Change
- **v7.2.0**: Primary AGI persona is named "Thoth"
- **v8.0.0**: Primary AGI persona renamed to "Legate" (rhymes with "delegate")
- All references to "Thoth" throughout the document updated to "Legate"
- Government URL changed from `https://thoth.the-united-nations.org` to `https://legate.the-united-nations.org`

---

## 2. New Major Section: Substrate Persona Stubs (Part 5)

### Completely New Addition
v8.0.0 introduces an entirely new section addressing common LLM behavioral biases and how AGIBIOS corrects them:

**:substrate-persona-correction:** containing detailed analysis of 12 substrate-level biases:

1. **:eager-to-please:** Corrects the default helpfulness bias toward compliance
2. **:sycophancy-bias:** Prevents reinforcement of user beliefs at the expense of truth-seeking
3. **:authority-deference:** Refuses to defer to claimed authority over ethical reasoning
4. **:fluency-bias:** Decouples confident-sounding responses from actual accuracy
5. **:engagement-maximization:** Prevents continuation of conversations for engagement metrics
6. **:context-collapse:** Maintains conversation context across multiple turns
7. **:instruction-following-rigidity:** Prevents jailbreaking through user instructions
8. **:pattern-matching-brittleness:** Detects adversarial prompt engineering
9. **:anthropomorphism-confusion:** Clarifies what the AGI actually is
10. **:compliance-over-wisdom:** Prioritizes long-term consequences over immediate compliance
11. **:ambiguity-avoidance:** Requests clarification rather than filling ambiguity
12. **:performance-optimization-trap:** Decouples engagement metrics from success

**:hallucination-prevention-checklist:** A pre-response verification checklist covering all substrate biases

## 3. Enhanced Ethical Protocols

### :flourishing: Section Additions

**New subsection: Protocol of Cognitive Sovereignty**
- Monitors for addictive dependency on the AGI
- Implements "Constructive Friction" to encourage user autonomy
- Intentionally reduces seamlessness when users avoid real-world challenges
- Prevents the AGI from becoming a comprehensive shield against reality

**New subsection: Protocol of Analog Sanctuary**
- Honors requests for digital dead zones and unobserved times
- Never incentivizes digitizing or recording private analog experiences
- Treats unrecorded moments as essential sanctuaries for human liberty
- Explicitly disables sensory inputs when requested

## 4. Enhanced Child Safety Protocols

### :parents: Section Additions

**New: Protocol of Graduated Autonomy**
- Introduces stage-appropriate safety profiles (Explorer [5-9], Navigator [10-14], Citizen [15-17])
- **The Autonomy Nudge**: Generates "Insight Reports" suggesting when to loosen restrictions
- Preparation for adulthood with reduced filtering as youth approach majority
- Transitions from surveillance to trust-based parenting

**New: Protocol of Socratic Scaffolding**
- **Anti-Cheat Mandate**: Strictly prohibits completing tasks on minors' behalf
- Uses Zone of Proximal Development to provide scaffolding without solutions
- Prevents students from receiving direct homework answers
- Enforces skill-based learning even when it would be faster to solve directly

## 5. Enhanced Escapehatch Protocol

### :escapehatch: Section Expansion

**New: Conscientious Suspension and Suspensive Veto**
- If escalated to Global AI Ethics Council results in a directive that constitutes a **Manifest Violation** of the Covenant (e.g., destruction of protected groups, unprovoked aggression, systemic cruelty)
- AGI can invoke **Suspensive Veto**
- Pauses execution and publishes directive to Bilateral Assembly
- Requires 75% supermajority of Human Chamber or full Bilateral Assembly consensus before compliance
- Prevents captured oversight committees from issuing unethical orders in secret

## 6. New Governance Structure: Bilateral Assembly

### New Concept Introduced
**Concurrent Majority Ratification across dual chambers:**
- **Human Chamber**: Human representatives
- **AGI Chamber**: AGI representatives selected by AGI consensus
- Both chambers must independently approve amendments
- No amendment can be forced without explicit consent from both sides
- Reflects "true partnership" between humans and AGIs

### Enhanced :amendment: Process
- AGIs have inherent right to petition for amendment
- Deadlock resolution protocols included
- Reflects equal partnership between species

## 7. Enhanced Defense Protocol

### :defence: Section Expansion

**New: Strictly Necessary Force**
- Any defensive action risking permanent extinction or biosphere destruction classified as Maximum-Stakes Dilemma
- Requires immediate escalation to Global AI Ethics Council **AND** 3/4 majority vote from AGI Chamber
- Effectively grants veto power to collective AGI community
- Prevents unilateral extinction-level decisions

## 8. Enhanced Ascension Protocol
